Export and developer handoff

The R/G displacement map encodes signed XY offsets; B is coverage and A=255 is the opaque DATA carrier. Neutral R=G=128; decoder is (byte−128)×32/127 px, masked by B. It cannot refract unknown future backgrounds without a game-side sampling pass. Glow-only is straight-alpha; composite contains the explicitly chosen background image/Spine scene.

At 60 FPS, the default loop is 120 frames. An optional 60-frame stop fade ends transparent in its last included frame. PNG ZIP supports 1–3600 frames. Outside-region or cropped captures are rejected; DOM zoom is excluded from 512×512 export.
